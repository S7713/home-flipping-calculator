iPhone / iPad setup: Open this calculator at its HTTPS website address in Safari, not as a downloaded HTML preview in Files. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App (if offered), then tap Add. Open the new Home Screen icon at least once while online to prepare offline access. You can then use the calculator offline after the files have cached. Projects save only on that device and app/browser profile; use Back Up All Projects regularly. If an offline page does not load, reconnect and reopen the app once.

How to Use It

1. Click My Projects to see every property, or New deal to create an investment scenario, or edit the included example.

2. Enter property, financing, selling, and holding assumptions on Deal Inputs.

3. Add your renovation estimates on Rehab Budget.

4. Review the summary cards and use Sensitivity Analysis to stress-test the deal.

5. Use Back Up All Projects to download a portable JSON backup for every property. Use Restore Backup to restore it. Export CSV for spreadsheets or Print / PDF for a client-facing summary.

This product does not collect or send financial data anywhere. Images are illustrative; numbers are your estimates.

Maximum offer: the highest purchase price estimated to meet your target profit and safety buffer, including any purchase-price-dependent closing fees and automatic financing. Taxes are excluded.

ROI: estimated net profit divided by estimated upfront cash required. Annualized ROI is a simple annualization, not IRR. No income tax or capital gains tax calculation is included.
